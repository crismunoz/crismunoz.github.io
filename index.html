I build tools and frameworks that make AI systems more trustworthy, fair, and explainable. My work spans LLM evaluation and benchmarking, bias mitigation, model explainability, and responsible AI — from designing metrics and open-source libraries to scaling transformer models across 100+ GPUs.

Over 12+ years I've moved between research, engineering, teaching, and consulting — leading R&D teams, publishing 15+ papers at venues like NeurIPS, teaching NLP and deep learning at universities in Brazil and Peru, and advising organizations including the International Labour Organization. Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering. Trilingual: Spanish, English, Portuguese.
